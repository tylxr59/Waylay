Merchant's Favor: crate fills

Each Waylaid Crate needs one of its listed materials. A row is one way to fill one crate: buy the crate and the materials, then turn it in. White crates (raw materials) pay 5/10/15/20 Favor by tier. Green crates (bars, parts, bolts and hides) pay 10/20/30/40. vs usual compares today's material price with TSM's historical price.

Reputation: Craftsman's Writs

Buy the writ on the AH, buy (or craft) the requested item, and hand it in at the writ's NPC. Net cost = writ + item − the average Coin Pouch. Each writ is once per day. Rep shown is before the Legacy bonus.
